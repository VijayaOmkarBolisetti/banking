Delivery plan

Realistic phases and timelines

Timelines start from the date vendor contracts and API access are confirmed — not from today. Phases can run in parallel where dependencies allow.

✓

Now — Demo ready

Live today

Full customer app, revolving credit line, EMI payment, and lender admin controls. Available at banking-flax-three.vercel.app.

1

Phase 1 — DigiLocker KYC, photos, accept and validate

4 – 8 weeks

DigiLocker PAN and Aadhaar fetch, selfie and document photo upload, review and accept screen, system validation. Vendor: Setu / HyperVerge / Signzy.

2

Phase 2 — Penny-drop, eSign, UPI Autopay

3 – 6 weeks

₹1 IMPS bank verification, Aadhaar eSign on the loan agreement, UPI Autopay or eNACH mandate. Vendor: Razorpay or Cashfree, and Leegality or SignDesk.

3

Phase 3 — Bureau, rules engine, live disbursal

6 – 10 weeks

CIBIL / Experian / CRIF pull, custom approve / reject / refer rules, live IMPS or NEFT disbursal. Requires an NBFC entity or co-lending partner first.

4

Phase 4 — Collections, WhatsApp, white-label, MIS

4 – 6 weeks

DPD dashboard, overdue auto-block, WhatsApp and SMS reminders, white-label brand and domain, board MIS and export reports.

* Estimates from the date API access and vendor contracts are signed. Bureau and disbursal depend on NBFC or regulatory approval secured independently by the client.

CreditFlow 05
Client-side dependencies

What we need from you

These decisions sit on the client side. The sooner each is confirmed, the sooner the matching phase can go live.

Regulatory and legal

  • NBFC license or co-lending partner for real credit
  • Loan agreement template and privacy policy
  • KYC and data processing policy (RBI guidelines)

KYC vendor

  • DigiLocker partner onboarding (MeitY / UIDAI)
  • Confirm: PAN + Aadhaar both required, or PAN only
  • Photo rules: selfie only, or selfie and document image

Bureau access

  • CIBIL / Experian / CRIF membership or aggregator
  • Regulated entity credentials required for bureau pull
  • Confirm which bureau or bureaus are required

Payments

  • Payment gateway account (Razorpay / Cashfree / PayU)
  • eSign vendor (Leegality / SignDesk / NSDL)
  • Bank account for IMPS and NEFT disbursal
This meeting

Decisions needed

  • DigiLocker — Aadhaar mandatory, or optional alongside PAN
  • Failed KYC — auto-reject or manual review by ops in admin
  • White-label branding (brand colours, domain)
  • Target go-live date for Phase 1
  • NBFC partner name or own license status

Bottom line

Every feature in the pitch is technically feasible and used in live Indian lending products today. The demo proves the experience works end to end. Live rails plug into the same screens once vendor contracts and approvals are in place. Nothing needs to be rebuilt.
